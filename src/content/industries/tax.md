## What Is the Tax Industry?

The tax profession helps individuals, businesses and other organisations understand and comply with tax rules. Depending on the role, professionals may calculate tax liabilities, prepare filings, advise on transactions, interpret legislation or support tax policy and administration.

Tax is closely connected to accounting, law, finance and economics, but it has its own technical knowledge and regulatory requirements.

## What Does Tax Work Look Like in Real Life?

- Preparing and reviewing tax returns or filings.
- Analysing the tax implications of business transactions.
- Helping organisations meet filing and payment obligations.
- Researching tax legislation and guidance.
- Supporting tax audits and responding to enquiries.
- Advising on tax efficient structures within applicable laws.

## Key Terminology

- **Taxable income:** Income calculated under the applicable rules for tax purposes.
- **VAT:** A consumption tax applied under a defined system to relevant supplies.
- **Tax return:** A filing reporting relevant information to a tax authority.
- **Transfer pricing:** The pricing of transactions between related entities, subject to applicable rules.
- **Tax compliance:** Meeting relevant filing, reporting and payment obligations.
- **Tax residency:** A status determined under the applicable rules that may affect tax obligations.
- **Tax avoidance and tax evasion:** These terms have different meanings. Their treatment depends on the relevant legal framework. Never assume that a tax arrangement is lawful simply because it reduces tax.
